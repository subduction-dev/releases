### New

- Workflows can now include a decision step, where a model chooses one answer from a fixed list of choices you define, and the run follows the route for that choice. The choices, their probabilities and the model use are recorded, so you can see why a run went the way it did.
- Cases can now be created as sample cases on request, up to ten at a time for one case type, through the API. Each comes with its record, a timeline, and decisions, and is marked Sample. The Cases list badges sample and test-run cases and offers Remove sample cases… to delete the sample ones together with everything filed under them.
- Admin has a new Plugins page. It lists each plugin with its version, whether it runs and its settings, and lets an administrator switch it on or off.

### Improved

- Reviewing a draft or proposal in Policies now highlights only the documents and workflow steps that changed, so unchanged ones stay out of the way. Generated documents stay read-only until you accept a suggestion to edit them.
- A policy can now hold several drafts at once without losing an older one. Every draft is listed on the policy, including policies you wrote yourself rather than installed from a pack.
- Usage in AI Management now counts what sample cases cost under Cases, together with chat.

### Fixed

- Installing a pack in Policies could occasionally fail while its entities were still being prepared. The install now waits long enough to finish.

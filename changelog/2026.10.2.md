### New

- A What's new button, marked with a gift icon, now sits at the top of the right-hand dock above Chat and Notifications. It shows the version you're using and opens that version's entry in the changelog.
- When a newer version is available, the What's new button shows a dot and offers to reload the page. Nothing reloads on its own.

### Improved

- The changelog has a new look, in both light and dark mode. It now also covers earlier releases.

### Fixed

- After an update, opening an area that needs the newer version no longer reloads the page without warning or shows the crash screen. Instead, the area asks you to reload, and What's new opens beside it.
- An answered decision no longer appears twice on a case's timeline. The question stays where it was asked, with who answered it, when and what they chose. The full answer appears once, at the time it was made.

### New

- Each organization now has its own web address, so a link or bookmark always opens in the organization it belongs to. If you belong to more than one organization, you can keep each one open in its own browser tab, and each tab stays in its own organization.
- Drafts and your current chat conversation are kept separate for each organization, so text you type in one organization's tab doesn't appear in another's.

### Improved

- Switching organizations now updates the page address. Links and bookmarks from before this change still open the same page in the right organization. A link to an organization you don't belong to now shows a page where you can pick one of your own.

### Fixed

- Creating two blank workflows at the same moment no longer fails. New blank workflows now get a short random ending on their name.

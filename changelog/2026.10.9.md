### New

- You can now build workflows directly in Workflows. Drafts are runnable from the start, you can rehearse them with realistic cases, and a case timeline is shown alongside so you can review how a rehearsal went.
- While building a workflow, the builder can also create the entity types, case types, reusable workflows and prompts it needs.
- Human input forms in workflows now show structured fields and repeated items, so you no longer have to enter JSON.

### Improved

- Applying many edits to a large workflow draft is much faster.

### Fixed

- A workflow function that runs out of time while starting up now reports that it exceeded its time budget, instead of showing a garbled setup error.
- Security hardening.

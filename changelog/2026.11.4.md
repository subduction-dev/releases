### New

- Cases can now hold several separate conversations, with the requester, an outside party, or staff only. Each has its own participants, channel and response state (open, awaiting a response, closed), and older messages stay readable in a conversation labelled legacy.
- Replies on a case are now reviewed by a person against the destination they were shown, and saved before they are sent. The case records what the provider reported (started, accepted, failed, uncertain, delivered or bounced) and never shows acceptance as delivery. A person checks any uncertain or stuck reply and records what they found.
- Workflows can now create a conversation on a case, attach a message, record what happened to a send, set the response state, and find a conversation by its provider thread.
- On a case's timeline, each card for a workflow AI step now has two closed sections, "Prompt sent" and "Response received". They show what the model was sent and every answer it gave, and only people with sensitive-data access can open them.
- Creating or updating a Zendesk ticket in a workflow can now set the ticket's brand and form, and a ticket read back includes both. If you leave them out, your Zendesk account defaults apply.

### Improved

- The workflow builder now plans its rehearsals more carefully, prepares its test data, and reports what a rehearsal did and did not prove.
- Marking a case as a test no longer allows a real external message to be sent. Rehearsals have their own reply path, which simulates the send.
- Install a pack in Policies now reads the list of published packs from a new source. If access to it is refused, you see "This deployment's access to the pack catalog was refused."

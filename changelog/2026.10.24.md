### New

- You can now ask the assistant to make sample cases, for example "make 3 sample bid intake cases". Each one comes with its record, a backdated history and decisions, and is marked Sample. If your request doesn't say which case type or how many, the assistant asks first. You can remove sample cases along with the others from the Cases list.

### New

- You can now add an OpenRouter key under Model keys in Settings, alongside OpenAI and Anthropic, and use it to run the assistant's models. OpenRouter is optional, and OpenAI and Anthropic keys work as before.

### Improved

- Answers in Slack, and workflow AI steps that have no model chosen, now use a model from a provider your organization has added model keys for. Workflows published before this release keep the model they were published with.

### Fixed

- Account and organization dialogs are now solid in dark mode. Before, the page behind them showed through and made them hard to read.
- Workflows now display correctly in light mode. The editor's top bar, canvas, side panel and step titles all follow the light theme, and a few small color problems in Cases and Entities are also fixed.
- When you ask the assistant about an area your window isn't connected to, it now says it can't reach that area. Before, it said there was nothing there, or gave no answer at all.
- If the assistant finishes a reply without writing anything, it now tries once more. If it still has nothing, chat and Slack show the usual notice that the reply failed, instead of going quiet.

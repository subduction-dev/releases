### Improved

- When Subduction on the web is told to slow down, it now waits and retries by itself instead of failing. A small notice in the top corner counts down the seconds, then your request is sent again. If you are on a shared office network, it reads "Subduction is getting a lot of requests from your network right now. It will try again in N seconds."
- When part of Subduction fails to load because of that kind of slowdown, you now see the same countdown and the page reloads on its own. Before, it could wrongly show the screen saying Subduction may have been updated.

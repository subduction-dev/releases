### New

- Policies are now grouped by zone, with a rail on the left. A policy appears under each zone its procedures belong to, and under Unassigned if it has none. You can create and edit zones from Policies, switch between list and grid, search, and filter by Active or Archived. The link you copy reproduces your view, and each tile in the grid shows the policy's stages.
- Request cards now accept files by upload, by paste, or by dragging an existing file from a case's sidebar. A dropdown offers the same choice of existing files. Removing a file only removes it from that answer, and recommendations can have an exclusion checkbox with a reason.
- Decision reviews can now group related questions into cards. Answers keep their meaning, existing reviews look as they did, and a validation error moves focus to the question that needs attention.
- The policy builder now writes a short build plan for a new policy, then builds and sequences it one stage at a time. Every path ends in a result or in a task for a person that carries what was gathered. After an AI step, a check looks only at the shape of the output and hands the work to a person who can accept, fix or redo it, so approving a draft is always possible.

### Improved

- Opening an active case now takes you to its pending request or active step. New activity follows into view while you are following current work. If you are reading history or editing a form, your position stays put and a New activity button lets you jump ahead.
- Case timeline navigation leaves room below the sticky header, briefly highlights the card you land on, and no longer adds excessive blank space after the last entry.
- The policy builder now produces fewer and shorter documents: one policy for staff, one procedure for each main workflow, reference documents only where a source must be kept, and a single build log. Customer or public pages are written only when you ask for them.
- Published policy documents are sorted into Policy, Internal procedures, Help center and Reference, and Organize can override that. A policy or procedure that runs long gets a "worth a look" note in review, which never blocks anything.
- The builder places each workflow in its category and zone as soon as it creates it. Its replies are shorter and lead with what changed.
- Policy authors can review more of a policy as one proposal, including case stages. They can withdraw workflows they no longer need and are no longer held to 32 workflows.
- Previews of Markdown and plain-text files now render in place. Tables in PDFs and Markdown size their columns to their content and wrap long values without splitting header words.
- Case files can be embedded in documents, memos can be compared side by side, and the editor's slash menu can pick a file for review.
- The active tab and conversation are kept in the page address, so a link or a reload returns you to the same place. Carried working notes stay visually distinct, and empty decision receipts no longer appear in the timeline.

### New

- You can now ask the assistant in Chat or in Slack to make sample data, for example five sample records of an entity type. It creates real records marked Sample, written to fit the entity type and your organization's description. If you leave out the type or the number, the assistant proposes one and waits for your yes.

### Fixed

- The assistant now reads the fields of an entity type when you ask about it.
- Chat's standing notice no longer says the assistant never creates anything.
- Clearing "About your organization" in Admin's Settings, under Generation, now confirms that the description was removed. Before, it said the description was saved.

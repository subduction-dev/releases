### New

- Chat now knows which case or document you're looking at when you send a message. You can ask "tell me about this case" on a case, then move to a document and ask about that, all in the same conversation. Each message also remembers what you were looking at when you sent it, so you can refer back to "the case we discussed" later.
- A new panel on the right side of the window holds Chat, Notifications and a case's panels, so they're all in one place.

### Improved

- "Ask about this case" now opens Chat with the case already selected, so you can start typing right away. Chat replaces the separate chat inside Cases, and conversations you started there still open, as read-only.
- The assistant can now answer questions about a case's timeline and about entity types. It also shows dates and times in a readable form.

### New

- Policies lets you install a pack, a working policy you then make your own. An administrator sets up company facts once in "Set up your company", and you review the policy's documents and its workflows together as a draft before publishing. Published policies can be maintained, exported, updated or archived from the same area.
- Workflows can now make sample records with a new "Make sample records" action, up to 50 records of an entity type per run. The model cost of these records appears in Usage under System.

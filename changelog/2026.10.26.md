### New

- Home now has a "Recent" section listing the cases touched most recently, one row per case.
- Pressing `?` opens a list of the keyboard shortcuts Subduction supports. You can also reach it from the "Keyboard shortcuts" row in the ⌘K launcher. Typing a question mark in a field still types one.
- Case types now appear in the ⌘K launcher, so you can jump to one the same way you can from the sidebar.

### Improved

- Failed cases now show up in Home's "Needs attention" next to the cases marked for attention.
- Links that point to a record by its id, such as a document, now open the right page in Subduction whichever area the record belongs to. If nothing matches the link, you see a short message instead of a blank page.

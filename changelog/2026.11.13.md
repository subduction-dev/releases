### New

- **Workbench on cases:** A case now opens in Workbench, where its activity, open questions and conversations read as one chat. A composer at the bottom sends what you write to the right place.
- **A brief of where the case stands:** Workbench shows a short brief, with each sentence pointing to the entries it rests on. It updates whenever the case moves.
- **The assistant can prepare actions for you:** In Workbench, the assistant can prepare a stage move, an update to the requester or an answer. Nothing happens until you confirm it, and it can never do more than you could.
- **Tinted conversations:** Each conversation on a case has its own colour, so you can tell the threads apart.

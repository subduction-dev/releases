### Improved

- Chat with Anthropic models now reuses the parts of a conversation that have not changed from one step to the next, so the same work costs less. In Admin → AI Management, Usage counts that cached input separately and prices it at the cache rate.

### Fixed

- When you ask the assistant what is new, it now starts from your most recent cases instead of the oldest. Chat answers in Cases, Documents and Workflows also say more plainly when a list was cut short and how to see the rest.
- When a model declines to answer, the run now ends as failed with "the model declined to answer this request" instead of saving a half-written reply. The same applies to answers in Slack.
- The Reasoning block above a chat answer now fills in for Anthropic models, which until now left it empty.
- A case's timeline no longer looks complete after the first page when more entries exist.

### New

- You can now assign cases to people and manage who is responsible for a case from Cases.
- You can collapse the main navigation to icons. Hovering over an icon opens a flyout menu with that area's pages.
- You can resize sidebars by dragging their edges.

### Improved

- Documents now show tables and task lists in document text.
- Navigation, titles and notifications now name each area the same way throughout Subduction: Cases, Documents, Workflows, Entities, Notifications and Chat.
- Pages use consistent content widths, and your profile photo now appears in Subduction.

### Fixed

- Security hardening.
- Paired buttons, such as Save and Cancel, are now the same height and line up.
- The case view no longer shows a second Chat row. It also has an "All events" option, leaves room at the bottom of the page, and shows a clear card when a case has no chat yet.
- Chat shown inside another area now matches that area's theme.
- Opening a large number of documents at once no longer fails.

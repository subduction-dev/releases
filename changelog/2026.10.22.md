### Improved

- The policy builder's documents and rehearsal progress now update on screen as they change.

### Fixed

- Policies now shows a plain "not found" message, instead of a server error, when a link points to a policy package, workflow or other item that doesn't exist.
- Creating and building a policy is more reliable. Chat choices stay clickable after the assistant reaches its work limit, and Archive policy stays on the policy's main page.
- Private rehearsals can now read the document version they wrote or one an analyst approved.
- Chat no longer reports a turn as failed while it is still running.
